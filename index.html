Privacy Policy — Fair

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Fair ("the App") is developed and operated by Alexander Litovchenko, an individual developer based in Germany (Apple Developer Team ID: SFQY7V73AL), who is the controller for the data described here. This policy explains what data the App processes, why, and what choices you have. If you have any questions, contact litovchenko.alexanders3@gmail.com.

Fair has no user accounts, no passwords and no registration. When the App first connects to our backend, it creates an anonymous technical ID for your device. This ID is not linked to your name, email or phone number; it is only used to decide which shared groups this device may open, and to limit abuse (see the sections below).

1. Data stored only on your device

The following stays on your phone. It is permanently deleted if you uninstall the App or use "Delete all data" in Settings:

  • Personal budget entries, category limits, income and recurring personal transactions (Budget tab)
  • App preferences (language, theme, accent color, currency display, which changelog version you've seen)
  • Groups you never turned syncing on for, with all their expenses

Your display name, profile photo and payment methods are also stored on your device. If you are a member of a synced group, they are copied to your member entry in that group (see Section 2).

2. Data stored for groups you choose to sync

You can turn on syncing for a group so that everyone in it sees the same expenses. The data is then stored on our backend, Supabase, which acts as our data processor; the servers are in London (United Kingdom, a country with an EU adequacy decision). Only devices that have joined the group with its invite code or link can read or change it; knowing the code alone shows only the group's name and number of members before joining. After 20 wrong codes within one hour, further tries are blocked for a while.

3. AI-assisted features (Anthropic)

Two optional features send data to Anthropic (maker of Claude), an AI provider based in the United States, only to produce a result for you — never for advertising or profiling:

  • Receipt scanning: the photo you choose is sent to read the amount, date and shop name. The result is only a suggestion that you check before saving.
  • Trip summary: the group name, totals, number of days and expenses, the largest category, the name and amount of the person who paid the most, and the description and amount of the largest expense are sent to write a short summary. No photos are sent.

Both features are optional; Fair works fully without them. To prevent abuse, the number of uses per day is counted per anonymous device ID (or, without one, per IP address). This involves a transfer to the United States, covered by Anthropic's data processing terms, which include the EU standard contractual clauses. Anthropic does not use data sent through its API to train its models.

4. Weather, maps and places

If you set a place for a group or an expense, Fair sends the place name you type, or the coordinates, to Open-Meteo to find the place and show the current weather. Finding a place from your current location and showing expenses on a map use Apple's location and map services on your iPhone. Location access is only used, with your permission, when you add a place yourself; Fair never tracks your location in the background.

5. Push notifications (Expo)

If you allow notifications, your device's push token is stored (see Section 2) and used, through Expo's push service and Apple's notification service, to tell you about new expenses and payment reminders in a synced group. To deliver a notification, its text is passed to these services: the group name, the name of the person who paid or sent the reminder, the expense description and the amount.

6. Currency conversion (Frankfurter)

If an expense is in another currency than the group's, Fair looks up the exchange rate from Frankfurter, a public exchange-rate service. Only the two currency codes are sent (e.g. "EUR" and "USD").

7. Crash and error reporting (Sentry)

Fair uses Sentry to catch crashes and errors so they can be fixed. It is configured not to send personal data: no user ID, no default personal data, and IBANs, email addresses and invite codes are masked before a report is sent. A report contains technical information such as the code that was running and the device model.

8. Language statistics and translation suggestions

To see whether each app language works well, Fair counts a few anonymous events per language, for example "onboarding finished" or "switched to English". Only the day, the language, the event and a count are stored — no user, device or group. If you use Settings → Language → Improve Translation, Fair sends the language, the text you picked, your suggestion and your optional note, together with the anonymous device ID (only to count how many different people suggest the same change and to limit abuse). Suggestions are deleted after 180 days at the latest.

9. How long data is kept

  • Data on your device: until you delete it, use "Delete all data", or uninstall the App.
  • Synced group data: as long as the group exists. Deleting the group in the App removes the group, its members, expenses and reminders from our backend. Photos stay reachable by their link until we remove them; ask us and we will.
  • Leaving a group removes your device's access and push token; your member entry (name, photo, payment methods) stays in the group so the others' balances stay correct. Ask us if you want it removed or anonymized.
  • "Delete all data" in Settings deletes the data on your device. It does not yet delete your member entries in synced groups on our backend; write to us and we delete them.
  • The record of wrong invite codes is deleted automatically after one day, the daily AI usage counters after 30 days, translation suggestions after 180 days.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data and to object to its processing. Most data you control directly in the App. For anything else — for example data in a group you can no longer open — write to litovchenko.alexanders3@gmail.com; this is a one-person project, so requests are handled personally. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the one in your country.

11. Children

Fair is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the date above changes, and significant changes are noted in the App's changelog.

Nähdäkseen, toimiiko kukin sovelluksen kieli hyvin, Fair laskee kielikohtaisesti muutamia anonyymeja tapahtumia, esimerkiksi ”käyttöönotto suoritettu” tai ”vaihdettu englantiin”. Vain päivä, kieli, tapahtuma ja lukumäärä tallennetaan — ei käyttäjää, laitetta eikä ryhmää. Jos käytät kohtaa Asetukset → Kieli → Improve Translation, Fair lähettää kielen, valitsemasi tekstin, ehdotuksesi ja valinnaisen muistiinpanosi yhdessä anonyymin laitetunnisteen kanssa (vain sen laskemiseksi, kuinka moni eri henkilö ehdottaa samaa muutosta, ja väärinkäytön rajoittamiseksi). Ehdotukset poistetaan viimeistään 180 päivän kuluttua.

9. Kuinka kauan tietoja säilytetään

  • Laitteellasi olevat tiedot: kunnes poistat ne, käytät toimintoa ”Poista kaikki tiedot” tai poistat sovelluksen.
  • Synkronoitujen ryhmien tiedot: niin kauan kuin ryhmä on olemassa. Kun poistat ryhmän sovelluksessa, ryhmä, sen jäsenet, kulut ja muistutukset poistetaan palvelimeltamme. Kuvat ovat saatavilla linkkinsä kautta, kunnes poistamme ne; kerro meille, niin poistamme ne.
  • Kun poistut ryhmästä, laitteesi käyttöoikeus ja push-tunnuksesi poistetaan; jäsentietosi (nimi, kuva, maksutavat) jäävät ryhmään, jotta muiden saldot pysyvät oikeina. Kerro meille, jos haluat, että ne poistetaan tai anonymisoidaan.
  • Asetusten ”Poista kaikki tiedot” poistaa tiedot laitteeltasi. Se ei vielä poista jäsentietojasi synkronoiduista ryhmistä palvelimeltamme; kirjoita meille, niin poistamme ne.
  • Luettelo vääristä kutsukoodeista poistetaan automaattisesti yhden päivän kuluttua, päivittäiset tekoälyn käyttölaskurit 30 päivän kuluttua ja käännösehdotukset 180 päivän kuluttua.
